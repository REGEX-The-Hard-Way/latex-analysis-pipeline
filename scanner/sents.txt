Identifying and understanding bounds on physical properties is important from the point of view of fundamental physics, predictions for theory and experiment, as well as searching for and rationalizing universal behavior   758032211  .
Properties for which bounds were recently discussed include viscosity and diffusivity.
The proposed   632748636   bounds for these two properties feature in a range of areas including, for example, strongly-interacting field theories, quark-gluon plasmas, holographic duality, electron diffusion, transport properties in metals and superconductors, and spin transport in Fermi gases   2675183149  .
Recently, two of us found a lower bound for the kinematic viscosity of liquids set by fundamental physical constants   3918824806  .
Here, we propose a new,   1163739713  , bound for the speed of sound in condensed matter phases in terms of fundamental constants.
Apart from setting the speed of elastic interactions in solids,   1329155215   is related to elasticity, hardness and affects important low-temperature thermodynamic properties such as energy, entropy and heat capacity   3103754819  .
As discussed below, the upper bound of   1329155215   sets the smallest possible entropy and heat capacity at a given temperature.
In solids,   1329155215   depends on elastic properties and density.
These strongly depend on the bonding type and structure which are inter-dependent   4016372219  .
As a result, it was not thought that   1329155215   can be predicted analytically without simulations, contrary to other properties such as energy or heat capacity which are universal in the classical harmonic approximation   3103754819  .
In view of this, representing the upper bound of   1329155215   in terms of fundamental constants is notable.
